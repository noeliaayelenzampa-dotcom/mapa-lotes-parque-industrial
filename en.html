Incentives

Incentives for companies

For companies setting up in the park for the first time and for those already here that expand.

Municipal

Industrial Promotion Regime · Ordinance No. 8560

  • 100%exemption from municipal taxes and fees for the first 5 years from final approval of the plant
  • 50%exemption for the following 5 years

Covers: general property fee (Tasa General Inmobiliaria), public works contribution (Tasa Solidaria para Obras Públicas), health, hygiene and safety inspection fee (Tasa por Inspección Sanitaria, Higiene, Profilaxis y Seguridad), community and tourism promotion fund, building permit fee (Derecho de Edificación) and administrative fees.

Provincial

Entre Ríos Industrial Promotion Regime · Law No. 11,071

  • 100%exemption for 15 years from provincial turnover tax (Ingresos Brutos), property tax, stamp tax and vehicle tax
  • 15% · 8% · 4%rebate on electricity consumption depending on the contracted tariff, plus 5% when renewable energy is used

The 15 years are 10 under the law plus 5 for locating in a recognised industrial park (sections 11 and 15). The turnover tax exemption does not cover sales to final consumers. Applications are filed with the Government of Entre Ríos, with a production project.

If you are already in the park and expand

Ordinance No. 8560 · sections 20 to 23

Up to 5 years of exemption on the increase in the health, hygiene and safety inspection fee generated by your new investments.

  • 100%of the increase, if you add at least 40% more staff and two of these: +50% output, +50% equipment or +50% built area
  • 50%of the increase, if you add at least 20% more staff and two of these: +30% output, +30% equipment or +30% built area

It must be requested before investing, detailing the investments and the timeframe. When the promotion period ends, a deferral of up to 50% of that fee for 5 years can also be requested, with similar requirements.

Incentives are granted by resolution of the municipal Executive, with the involvement of the Municipal Development Commission. They require a municipal tax clearance certificate and investments certified by a registered accountant. Check the conditions with the Industrial Park Office.

    Application

    How to set up in the park

    Municipal lots are awarded to industrial production projects.

    The process, step by step

    1. EnquiryContact the Industrial Park Office to review available lots, price and conditions.
    2. ApplicationSubmit the form with your project: initial investment, covered area, staff, environmental impact report for the activity and construction timeframe. It is filed at the Municipality's general registry (Mesa General de Entradas) with a letter addressed to the Mayor.
    3. AssessmentThe Municipal Development Commission reviews the project: investment, employment, contribution to industrial development and environmental impact. With its report, the Executive issues the award decree.
    4. Within 30 daysFrom notification of the decree: written acceptance of the lot, land survey and full payment or signing of the payment plan.
    5. Within 180 daysFrom notification: submission of the building project to the municipal Urban Inspection Office (Dirección de Fiscalización Urbana).
    6. 180 more daysFrom approval of the project: start of construction.
    7. Up to 24 monthsFrom handover of the lot: plant licensed and operating. The term may be extended for major investments.
    8. Title deedWith construction finished, the municipal licence obtained and the price paid, the deed is signed. Survey, plans and deed costs are borne by the company.

    What to submit

    • Completed application form, signed on every page (it has the status of a sworn statement)
    • Company details: legal name, address, type of company and directors
    • Articles of association, or deed of incorporation and bylaws, depending on the type of company
    • Investment project: amounts by category, production process, plant layout, capacity, raw materials and staff
    • Environmental impact report for the activity (Ordinance No. 8560, section 10)
    • Products to be made, estimated start-up date and destination of production (domestic market or export)
    • Capital and commercial and bank references
    • Existing companies: income tax return and latest balance sheet
    • Municipal tax clearance certificate. Companies with outstanding tax or social security debts cannot access the regime

    Information submitted by the company is kept confidential. Forms and regulations are in Spanish.

    Building rules

    • 1,250 m²minimum lot, with a minimum frontage of 25 m
    • 70%maximum of the lot occupied by works; the remaining 30%, landscaped
    • 25% to 40%of the lot, covered area at ground level
    • 5 msetback from the street line and from the other lot boundaries
    • 2.50 msidewalk along the streets

    Also: 2 m chain-link perimeter fence, paved loading and parking areas, lighting inside the lot. Housing is not allowed.

    A body that brings together the municipal Executive, the City Council and the companies in the park. It takes part in lot awards, industrial promotion and park planning.

    Association of Companies of the Industrial Park: more than 40 member industries. It runs a jobs portal that connects applicants with companies in the park.
